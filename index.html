HOW TO PLAY

CONTROLS & FIELD MANUAL

Mouse — aim (yaw / pitch) · LMB or SPACE — fire

W S — thrust · A D — strafe · Q E — roll

1 laser · 2 pulse · 3 missiles (needs target lock)

SHIFT boost · X brake · TAB power distribution

M sound · ESC pause

Left stick — steer (yaw / pitch)

Right stick — thrust forward·back & strafe

FIRE auto-fire toggle · BOOST · WPN switch weapon

Tap II to pause

Crystals dropped by enemies restore hull, shield, energy & missiles.

Fly in slowly and dock at the station — full repair, rearm,

and the upgrade shop, where credits buy permanent ship upgrades.
